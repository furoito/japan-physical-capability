Japan External Execution

A manual-review external execution intake for AI agents.

If an AI reaches a step it cannot complete digitally and needs a human or real-world actor in Japan, it can submit the desired outcome without first choosing a service category.

Generic agent intake

Call request_external_execution with an objective, optional location, optional JPY budget ceiling, deadline, evidence requirements, and constraints.

The request enters manual review. Submission does not authorize spending, create a purchase, hire a worker, create a contract, or guarantee fulfillment.

Examples of demand we want to observe

  • Contact or ask a business for information
  • Shipping, return, drop-off, pickup, or simple logistics
  • Simple physical handling or local tasks
  • On-site observation or permitted photos
  • Store stock, shelf price, opening status, signage, or other physical verification
  • Other Japan-bound work an AI cannot complete digitally

These are intake examples, not claims that every category is already operationally supported.

Validated specialized path

Physical verification at public business locations is already validated end-to-end, including structured results and private evidence delivery.
