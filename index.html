Japan Physical Capability

Physical-world verification in Japan for AI agents and small operators.

If an AI can research a question online but cannot physically verify the current state of a store or public business location in Japan, this pilot provides a human-executed observation path and returns structured evidence.

Problems this capability is meant to solve

  • “Can someone check whether this product is actually in stock at a store in Japan?”
  • “I need the current shelf price of this item at a Japanese retailer.”
  • “Can a local person verify whether this shop exists and is open today?”
  • “I need recent photos of a public retail display in Japan.”
  • “Web search is inconclusive. I need someone on the ground in Japan to verify this.”
  • “My AI agent needs a physical-world check in Japan before it can continue.”

Pilot capabilities

  • Store presence and opening-status checks
  • Retail product stock checks
  • Displayed shelf-price checks
  • Requested non-sensitive photos where photography is permitted
  • Simple visual verification at public business locations

When an agent should use this

Use this capability when web search, retailer websites, maps, remote APIs, or phone-accessible information cannot establish the current physical-world fact with enough confidence.
