Japan Physical Capability

A physical-world verification capability for AI agents.

Use this when an AI can research a question but cannot physically verify the current state of a public business location in Japan.

Pilot capabilities

  • Store presence / opening check
  • Retail product stock check
  • Displayed price check
  • Requested photos where permitted
  • Simple visual verification
